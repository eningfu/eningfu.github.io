Bee-Legal

Overview

A high-fidelity mobile app prototype designed for beekeepers of all experience levels. The app brings together beekeeping news, interactive maps, educational articles, videos, and other resources in one platform. The project focused on creating an intuitive interface, clear navigation, and a consistent user experience while organising a wide range of information.
